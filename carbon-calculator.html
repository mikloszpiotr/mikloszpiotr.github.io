Pareto Frontier — CO₂ Reduction vs Implementation Cost
ML Algorithm: Pareto Multi-Objective Optimisation
Each abatement action scored on CO₂_reduction and implementation_cost. Pareto frontier computed by iterating over action space and removing dominated solutions (where another action achieves more reduction at lower cost). Actions are ranked by abatement_cost = $cost / tCO₂_saved. The 80/20 rule: top 20% of actions deliver >60% of total reduction potential.
Science-Based Target Reduction Pathway — 2024 to 2030
ML Algorithm: SBT Trajectory Pathfinder
Linear decarbonisation pathway calculated from baseline to target year. Year-by-year required reduction derived as baseline x (1 - targetPct/100) ^ (year - baseYear) / (targetYear - baseYear). Gap analysis compares committed actions (from Pareto top picks) against required trajectory. Red zone = off-track years requiring accelerated intervention.
