ML Algorithm: K-Means Warehouse Placement
Warehouse positions initialised via K-Means++ seeding on weighted customer coordinates. Iterates centroid updates until convergence (delta < 0.001). Each customer assigned to nearest warehouse by Euclidean distance weighted by demand volume. K = number of warehouses set by slider.
ML Algorithm: Greedy Transport Optimiser
After K-Means placement, a greedy assignment loop minimises total transport cost by evaluating all warehouse-customer pairs ranked by cost-per-unit-demand. Capacity constraints applied per warehouse. Re-runs automatically after any disruption event to find next-best assignment.

ML Algorithm: Disruption Impact Scoring
For each warehouse, the optimiser runs a counterfactual simulation: removes the node, re-runs greedy assignment, and measures delta-cost and delta-coverage. Resilience score = 1 - (impact / max_impact). Nodes with score < 0.4 flagged as critical single points of failure.
